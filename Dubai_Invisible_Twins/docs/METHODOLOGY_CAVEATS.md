# Additional methodological clarifications

## Bootstrap uncertainty
The 95% percentile interval is obtained from 1,000 independent with-replacement resamples of the eligible sales within each of the two selected community-years. The statistic is the percentage difference of sample medians (higher divided by lower, minus one). It is a conditional estimate of sampling variability under an independent-sales resampling model. It does not account for spatial, temporal or building-level dependence; it does not establish causal mechanisms, nor does it guarantee that sales constitute a random sample of a wider market. An interval excluding zero is not evidence about why prices differ.

## Low-price flags
The four hollow-dot records are flagged only because their sale AED/m² is below 25% of the relevant community-year median. Their legal or economic transaction type is not established. The records remain in every calculation.

## Correlation
Guest ratings are from short-term rental listings, not homeowners. The Spearman correlation uses midranks for ties and is calculated across communities with at least five uniquely rated listings for the selected year. Correlation is descriptive and is sensitive to small sample size and selection effects.

## Final verified Twin DNA ladder
Five metrics only: median sale price per m², median flat area, median transaction value, price IQR and eligible sale count. All are calculated from validated 2023–2025 property transactions. Unsupported metro, venue, population, off-plan and mortgage ladder attributes have been removed. The ranking and interactions remain.
