05 / AN INDEPENDENT LENS

Does a higher price mean better guest ratings?

Explore guest ratings from a separate short-term rental dataset, matched geographically to Dubai communities. These are not ratings of the apartments sold. These are guest reviews, not homeowner satisfaction. Each listing contributes one rating per year, using its latest observed monthly snapshot.

HOW TO READ THE GUEST-RATING CHART CLICK TO HIDE / SHOW

Each circle = one community. Left to right: higher median recorded sale price per square metre. Bottom to top: higher mean guest star rating (out of 5). Larger circles represent more uniquely rated rental listings. Teal and coral outlines identify your chosen communities. Hover for counts and ratings. Click a circle to choose a comparison when the pair is eligible under the sale-matching rules. What the data cannot tell us

These are historical recorded sales, not current listings. We include short-term rental guest ratings from a separate Kaggle dataset, but these do not measure homeowner satisfaction. The comparison controls for selected flat characteristics but not building quality, views or amenities. We do not infer building quality or why prices differ.

Data sources and tools

Transactions: CS333 course Dubai property transaction dataset, filtered to eligible 2023–2025 one-bedroom sales. Course dataset repository ↗

Geography: supplied Dubai community GeoJSON boundaries, used for community geography and spatial matching. Exact original publication URL should be confirmed.

Guest ratings: separate Kaggle short-term rental monthly listings dataset (listings_monthly.csv). Guest reviews are not homeowner ratings. Source citation pending: original Kaggle page, author and license must be added by the team.

Tools: D3.js v7 ↗ for charts; MapLibre GL JS ↗ for the interactive map; Python for data preparation; HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the website.
